Building research infrastructure for understanding companies, markets and changing economic systems.

Vael Systems is an independent research and software-development project combining public data, artificial intelligence, quantitative methods and disciplined evidence testing.

VAEL PULSE

Company intelligence through measurable change.

Vael Pulse is an experimental company-intelligence research platform designed to identify meaningful changes in public company activity using point-in-time public information.

The project investigates changes in demand, attention, technology adoption, company fundamentals and peer-relative behaviour while preserving source provenance and uncertainty.

RESEARCH AREAS

Search & Attention

Studying changes in search demand, public attention and regional interest over time.

Company Intelligence

Comparing company behaviour against historical baselines and relevant peer groups.

Technology Adoption

Researching public indicators of developer, software and technology adoption.

Quantitative Research

Applying statistical and mathematical methods to test whether observed patterns contain reproducible information.

GOOGLE TRENDS RESEARCH

Measuring changes in public search behaviour.

Google Trends data is being evaluated as one component of Vael Pulse's multi-source research architecture.

Intended research includes brand-search momentum, peer-relative search behaviour, geographic changes in attention and longitudinal company monitoring.

Search activity is treated as research evidence rather than a standalone prediction or trading signal.

RESEARCH PRINCIPLES

Evidence before conclusions.

Vael Systems separates observation from validation. Public-data patterns and AI-generated hypotheses are research candidates until independently tested.

The project emphasises provenance, point-in-time data, reproducibility, uncertainty and controlled experimentation.
